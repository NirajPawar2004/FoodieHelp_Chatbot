# FOODIEHELP DELIVERY AND SERVICE COMMITMENT POLICY
Document ID: POL-DEL-003
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Version: 1.8

1. DELIVERY WINDOWS & ESTIMATIONS
- Estimated Delivery Time (EDT) is calculated using restaurant preparation times, real-time traffic conditions, and delivery partner availability.
- Typical delivery time ranges from 25 to 45 minutes for standard city radius (within 7 km).
- In cases of extreme weather (monsoon, heavy storms) or peak rush hours (lunch 1:00 PM - 2:30 PM, dinner 8:00 PM - 10:00 PM), estimated delivery times may be extended by 15-20 minutes.

2. ON-TIME DELIVERY GUARANTEE
- If a delivery arrives more than 30 minutes after the maximum Estimated Delivery Time due to our delivery network (and not customer unavailability), the customer is entitled to a Late Delivery Compensation Coupon of INR 100 or 10% credit towards their next order.
- Severe weather and declared municipal emergencies are exempt from compensation.

3. CONTACTLESS & SPECIAL INSTRUCTION DELIVERY
- Customers may request 'Contactless Delivery' at checkout. The delivery partner will place the parcel at the designated doorstep or security gate and notify the customer via notification or call.
- The delivery partner will attempt to call the customer a maximum of 3 times over a 10-minute waiting window. If the customer does not respond within 10 minutes, the parcel will be marked as 'Undelivered due to Customer Unreachable', and no refund will be provided.

4. WRONG OR INACCURATE ADDRESS
- Customers are responsible for providing precise GPS pin drops, building names, and apartment numbers.
- Once an order is 'Out for Delivery', the delivery address cannot be changed to a different zone exceeding 1 km from the original location.
