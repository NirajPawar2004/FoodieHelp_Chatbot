# FOODIEHELP WRONG ITEM RESOLUTION POLICY
Document ID: POL-WRG-005
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Version: 2.1

1. DEFINITION
A 'Wrong Item' issue occurs when an item delivered does not match what was ordered (e.g., received Veg Hakka Noodles instead of Chicken Biryani, or received a different beverage size, or violated explicit dietary dietary restrictions such as receiving a non-vegetarian dish when vegetarian was ordered).

2. CRITICAL DIETARY VIOLATIONS
- Dietary and religious safety are paramount. If a customer ordered a Vegetarian / Vegan / Halal / Jain meal and received a dish containing meat, egg, or animal derivatives:
  * The incident is immediately treated as a High Priority / Critical defect.
  * A 100% full order refund is initiated immediately.
  * A formal complaint is lodged against the restaurant partner.
  * An escalation ticket is generated for customer care review.

3. GENERAL WRONG ITEM RESOLUTION
- Time Limit: Issue must be reported within 24 hours of delivery.
- Information Needed: Order ID, the item that was expected, and description of the item received.
- Entitlement:
  * Option A: 100% refund for the value of the misdelivered item.
  * Option B: Free re-delivery of the correct item if kitchen is operational.
  * The customer is not required to return the incorrect food item.

4. REQUIRED NEXT ACTIONS
- Provide order ID and describe the incorrect item received.
- FoodieHelp support checks the invoice against restaurant catalog.
- If verified, refund or replacement ticket is confirmed instantly.
