# FOODIEHELP MISSING ITEM RESOLUTION POLICY
Document ID: POL-MIS-004
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Version: 2.0

1. DEFINITION
A 'Missing Item' incident occurs when one or more products listed in the confirmed order invoice and paid for by the customer are not found inside the sealed delivery package.

2. REPORTING REQUIREMENTS
- Time Limit: The missing item report must be logged within 24 hours from the delivery timestamp. Reports logged after 24 hours cannot be validated against restaurant dispatch logs and will be rejected.
- Order Details: The customer must provide the specific Order ID and identify the missing dish/item name(s).
- Package Condition: The customer must confirm if the restaurant tamper-evident seal was broken or intact upon arrival.

3. RESOLUTION OPTIONS
When a missing item is reported:
Option 1: Instant Item Refund (Default)
- A 100% refund for the cost of the missing item is issued back to the original payment method or FoodiePay Wallet.
- Pro-rated taxes corresponding to the missing item are included in the refund.

Option 2: Priority Re-order / Redelivery
- If the restaurant is still open and within operating hours, the customer can choose priority redelivery of the missing item with zero delivery fee.

4. INVESTIGATION & AUDIT
- Our automated merchant audit records the merchant's packing camera log. Repeat missing item incidents at a specific restaurant trigger a kitchen packing audit.
- Unsubstantiated repeated claims by an account may require manual support verification.
