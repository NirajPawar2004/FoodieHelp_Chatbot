# FOODIEHELP CUSTOMER REFUND POLICY
Document ID: POL-REF-001
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Version: 2.4

1. OVERVIEW & SCOPE
At FoodieHelp, we are committed to delivering fresh, accurate, and delightful meals. When expectations are not met due to restaurant mistakes, delivery delays, missing items, or transaction errors, customers may request a refund under the rules outlined below. All refunds are processed in the original mode of payment or credited instantly to FoodiePay Wallet based on customer preference.

2. REFUND ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
A customer is eligible for a full or partial refund under the following explicit conditions:

A. Missing or Incomplete Items:
- If one or more items from an order are missing, the customer is eligible for a 100% refund for the specific missing item(s), plus applicable pro-rated taxes.
- Requests must be submitted within 24 hours of delivery.

B. Wrong Item Received:
- If a restaurant delivers an entirely different dish or item, the customer is entitled to a 100% refund for the wrong item or a complimentary replacement redelivery.
- Photographic evidence or detailed description must be submitted within 24 hours of delivery.

C. Spoiled, Stale, or Quality Defect:
- If food delivered is unhygienic, uncooked, burnt, spilled excessively, or stale, customers may be granted a full or partial order refund after review by our quality team.
- Must be reported within 4 hours of delivery.

D. Order Cancellation by Customer:
- Order cancelled while status is 'Placed' (before restaurant begins food preparation): 100% full refund of total order amount.
- Order cancelled while status is 'Preparing' (restaurant has accepted and started cooking): 50% partial refund of total order amount to compensate for ingredients already utilized.
- Order cancelled while status is 'Out for Delivery' or 'Delivered': 0% refund. Cancellations are strictly not permitted once the rider is on the way.

E. Failed or Duplicate Payment Transactions:
- If an order transaction debited funds twice or the order failed to generate while money was deducted, a 100% refund is initiated automatically within 3 to 5 business days.

3. REFUND TIMELINES
- FoodiePay Wallet: Instant (within 15 minutes).
- UPI Payments: 24 to 48 hours.
- Credit / Debit Cards & Net Banking: 3 to 5 business days, subject to issuing bank processing schedules.

4. NON-REFUNDABLE SCENARIOS
- Customer provided an incorrect or unreachable delivery address.
- Customer was unavailable at the phone or door when the delivery partner arrived (after 10 minutes waiting window).
- Requests submitted after 24 hours of delivery.
- Subjective taste preferences (e.g., "dish was less spicy than expected") where the restaurant followed the standard recipe.
